# Claims policy for The Wall

Platform-wide rules live in the Radian repo's `CLAIMS.md` and apply here in full. Wall-specific
additions:

- **Phase 2 is a design, not a product.** Until the treasury contracts are deployed and audited,
  every mention of the NVDA pile, book value, or the buy wall is prefixed with "planned" or
  "in design". No numbers for it, ever, before there is a contract address to link.
- **"Floor" is a description of a mechanism, never a promise.** Write "a standing bid under the
  market price". Never "floor price", "price can't go below", "backed 1:1".
- **NVDA on testnet is a stand-in.** Say "NVDAx (testnet stand-in)" the first time on every
  page. It is not Nvidia stock, not a security, and has no redemption.
- **Book value ≠ redemption value.** If Phase 2 ships, book value is `treasury NVDA ÷
  circulating supply`, an on-chain ratio. Holding $WALL never gives a right to that NVDA.
- **Phase 1 mechanics are the platform's.** Buyback & Lock: bought-back $WALL is *locked* in
  the 5-year vault and vests 70% to the creator / 30% to the protocol. It is not burned.
- **No "undervalued".** The site never says $WALL is cheap, undervalued, or "below book",
  including in charts and tooltips.
- **Trades list = indexer data.** Label it as such; it can lag and excludes retired launches.
