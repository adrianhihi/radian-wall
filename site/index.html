Phase 2 · in design

Then the treasury becomes a wall

Phase 2 adds contracts that turn fee flow into a growing pile of NVDA and a floor under the price. Built from the same buyback-and-burn flywheel Radian already runs for its own token — proven on-chain, adapted here.

HOARD

The treasury accumulates NVDA

Creator-fee flow is redirected from a wallet to a treasury contract. It holds NVDA shares and never sells them. Treasury per token is the book value of $WALL — public, on-chain, every block.

DEFEND

A buy wall at the floor

When the market price drops toward book value, the treasury spends NVDA to buy $WALL back. That's the wall: a bid that's always there, funded by shares, not by hope.

COMPOUND

Buy back, burn, reward

Bought-back tokens are burned (book value per token rises) or streamed to stakers as NVDA-denominated yield. Less supply, more shares per token, every cycle.

Full mechanism, parameters, and open questions live in the product repo's TREASURY.md. It ships only after Arc mainnet, an audit of the new contracts, and a real tokenized stock to back it.
